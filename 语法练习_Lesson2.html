七、将以下单复数句进行转换
未批改
1.
将"This is a knife."改为复数句/单数句：
答案：These are knives.
this→these, is→are, knife→knives。
2.
将"That is a tomato."改为复数句/单数句：
答案：Those are tomatoes.
that→those, is→are, tomato→tomatoes。
3.
将"That child is very good."改为复数句/单数句：
答案：Those children are very good.
that→those, child→children, is→are。
4.
将"These are mice."改为复数句/单数句：
答案：This is a mouse.
these→this, are→is, mice→a mouse（单数还原）。
5.
将"Those are children."改为复数句/单数句：
答案：That is a child.
those→that, are→is, children→a child（单数还原）。
